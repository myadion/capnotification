# Changelog

## 1.0.0 — 2026-11-11

### New

- On-screen indicator for Caps Lock, Num Lock and Scroll Lock: a padlock at the bottom of the screen, closed when the key is on, open when it is off.
- Self-installing executable: starts at every sign-in, listed in the installed apps, `/silent` for unattended installs.
- Labels in English, French, Spanish, German, Italian, Portuguese and Dutch, chosen from the Windows display language.
- Turns off the Windows Toggle Keys beep and its shortcut.
